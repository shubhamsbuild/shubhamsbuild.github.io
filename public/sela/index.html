03 If I join

Three things I'd build at Sela.

1. An HMDA account board for the new strategic AE

Every non-bank lender's 2025 HMDA funnel (withdrawn, approved but not accepted, preapprovals not accepted) ranked against 139 peers and refreshed each filing year, so the BD hire opens every account with that lender's own number, like the NewDay email above.

2. A lender benchmark the founding marketer can launch

A public lookup: type a lender, see its HMDA funnel next to peers, full table gated. It turns into inbound from production and consumer-direct leads checking their own name, and gives webinars and conference dinners a data hook.

3. A trigger feed into the AE queue

A daily watch on channel acquisitions, product launches, agency pilots, executive moves and competitor deployments, each matched to the lender's HMDA numbers and routed to the owning rep with a drafted first line. Ten of those are on this page.
