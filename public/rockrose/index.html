Your moratorium year, and the renewal after it

Which senior communities sit in the 22 Gann Fire ZIP codes, protected until August 2027, and the hazard zone each faces after.

To: Owner or executive director of a senior living community, Skyline Place Senior Living (Milestone Retirement Communities)
Skyline Place's moratorium year

Skyline Place is in one of the 22 ZIP codes under the Gann Fire moratorium. Until August 2027, no insurer can cancel or non-renew its property policy solely because a wildfire occurred in the area. Senior living is named in the law, so a 135-bed campus isn't excluded by size.

The campus also sits in CAL FIRE's Very High hazard zone, so the renewal after that is the one to prepare for.

Your Firewise status, priced into the rate

Tahoe Donner's Firewise status since 2010, the rule that makes carriers price it, and the November filing that keeps it current.

To: General manager of a large HOA, Tahoe Donner Association
Firewise credit at your October renewal

Your 2027 budget adds about $56 per property for property and liability insurance. Tahoe Donner has been a Firewise USA site since 2010, and under California's Safer from Wildfires rule, any carrier whose rating plan prices wildfire risk has to reflect that in your rate.

The status counts as of the day your buildings are rated, and it stays current only through the November renewal filing.

Whether the Gann moratorium covers your winery

Which commercial policies in the 22 Gann Fire ZIP codes the moratorium protects, and which large ones it leaves out.

To: Owner of a winery, resort or venue, Ironstone Vineyards (Kautz Family Vineyards)
Ironstone and the Gann moratorium

Your Murphys estate is in one of the 22 ZIP codes under the Gann Fire moratorium. But the law leaves out commercial policies with $25,000 or more in annual premium where the insured averaged 25 or more employees, unless the property is residential.

A winery your size may fall outside it, and the estate sits in CAL FIRE's Very High hazard zone, so a wildfire-based non-renewal stays possible.
