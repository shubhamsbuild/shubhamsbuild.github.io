The Salesforce org traits behind your September sync failures

Which customer-org schema traits broke your Salesforce syncs in September, and the describe() check that catches each before indexing.

To: Founder or CTO, Onyx
Pre-flight checks for Salesforce orgs

Your three Salesforce sync failures in September came from customer schemas, not your code: objects with hundreds of custom fields overflowing a 16,384-byte request, a CreatedDate that's queryable but not sortable, and an entity that refuses DESC NULLS FIRST.
