Plan-check gap watch

Trigger
A modular, factory-built or Standard Plan rebuild permit changes status in LA City (submitted, corrections, resubmitted, approved, issued).
How
Weekly pull of the LADBS submitted-permits dataset, filtered to rebuild permits whose description says modular, factory built, prefab or Standard Plan. Read each permit page for the contractor or plan owner and the status history. Score by days sitting in the builder's turn (corrections to resubmittal) against the cohort median.
Output
Per account: the permit timeline table, the cohort benchmark, and a Message 1 draft for the owner, queued for rep review.
Cadence

Inspection-readiness watcher

Trigger
The same inspection item comes back Not Ready or No Access twice on one permit within 30 days.
How
Daily pull of the LADBS inspections dataset for rebuild permits. Group results by permit and item, flag repeats, then read the permit page for the contractor. Score by repeat count and by how many active permits that contractor holds.
Output
Per contractor: the inspection log by item and date, the 14.0% city benchmark, and a Message 2 draft, ready the day after the second miss.
