1. A weekly trigger feed for the NMHC 50

Every week, the owners and managers with a fresh event that reshapes a vendor book: REIT mergers and earnings filings, acquisitions, management takeovers, new asset-management leaders. Each arrives with the public line items and a first email, like the ten above.

2. Public-records audits as the first touch

For NYC and other open-data cities, a building- and device-level compliance list per operator (elevator, boiler, facade filings) matched to registrations, sent as the opener and turned into the sample audit Marc's 15-minute consultation already offers.

3. Speed-to-lead on demo requests

When someone books a demo, enrich the account within minutes: units and markets, recent deals, the expense lines in its filings, open compliance records. The founder walks into the call with a draft leakage hypothesis, and the follow-up is half written.
