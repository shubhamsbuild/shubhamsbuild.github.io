Your Florida retail rate on Ford recall and warranty labor

How to get Ford to pay recall and warranty labor at your Florida retail rate, with diagnosis time counted.

To: Fixed-ops lead or COO at a Ford store in Florida, Bartow Ford
Your retail rate on Ford recalls

When I checked on Oct 29, Ford had filed 18 recalls since the end of July. In Florida, section 320.696 lets you ask Ford in writing to pay recall and warranty labor at your retail customer-pay rate, and diagnosis time counts as labor.

With new owners since September, it's worth confirming which rate Ford has on file for Bartow. The math leaves out fleet, express and internal work, so Quick Lane and commercial ROs don't pull it down.

I turned the statute into a one-page request checklist. Want it?
