Your sepsis cases coded with an MCC, against your neighbors

How many more of your Medicare sepsis cases would carry an MCC at your neighbors' rate, and which two diagnoses to check.

To: Director of Clinical Documentation Integrity, Sutter Roseville Medical Center
Sepsis MCC capture in Roseville

In 2024, 78.6% of your Medicare sepsis cases were coded with an MCC. Mercy San Juan and the California median were both near 84%. At your volume, that gap is about 70 cases a year.

Two MCCs that often go undocumented in sepsis are severe malnutrition and metabolic encephalopathy. Both need real clinical support in the chart.
