## What it produced

**153 meetings booked** off a list built on a stand-in attribute, because the real
one cannot be bought. Outcome is unrecorded: every row still reads *scheduled*, so the defensible verb is *booked*, not *held*.

## The problem

The client described their customer as *business owners with personal taxable
income over $600,000*.

As a description of who pays them, that is exactly right. As an instruction for
building a list, it is impossible. **Personal income is not public and no database
sells it**, not because the vendors are lazy, but because the information does not
exist anywhere you can buy it.

The rest of the brief did not narrow things either. The industry was "most of
them", and three overlapping company-size brackets had been ticked, which between
them ruled almost nothing out.

## How I approached it

If you cannot find the thing itself, find something that **reliably comes with it**
and can actually be looked up.

The question becomes: what is true of nearly everyone earning over $600,000 as a
business owner, that somebody publishes a list of?

The answer was a licensed profession. Its members reliably clear that income
threshold, they are the right kind of business owner for this particular offer, and, most usefully of all, every state publishes a register of who holds that
licence. No vendor, no credits, no argument about coverage. A public list,
enumerable state by state.

## The part I would want to be asked about

I kept the original brief running.

A segment built on the ICP exactly as the client wrote it stayed live and kept
sending, **65,587 emails of it.**

That is the difference between an opinion and a result. Swapping out a client's
stated definition of their customer and simply asserting the new one is better is
not something you can defend when they ask. Keeping the old one running turns the
same decision into something measurable, and the answer was roughly **twice the
conversion rate** for the stand-in.

If it had gone the other way, I would have known that too, which is the point.

## What it did

**313 positive replies across 446,457 sends over eleven months**, with the control
still running underneath it the whole time.
