## Problem one: the customers are not in any database

A roofer with eleven trucks has no LinkedIn page and no job title worth filtering
on. The vendors that claim to cover this market are selling a thin slice of it.

![Four scattered public sources feed a scraper that finds directories first, producing about 60,000 trade companies and 27,000 professionals](/assets/exhibit-6-acquisition.svg)

**Solved by building it.** Map searches silently cap their results, so the grid
subdivides itself where a search comes back at the cap. One source blocked anything
that did not look like a real browser. Manufacturer registers turned out to carry
email addresses already.

## Problem two: we failed the first campaign

Not narrowly. **Three angles, 6,397 sends, three interested replies.**

![Seven verticals run in order; lawyers in Texas produced 28 of the 44 interested replies while three verticals produced none](/assets/exhibit-5-vertical-ladder.svg)

**Solved by changing the audience, not the words.** Three different arguments to
HVAC owners died together, which rules out the copy. Lawyers returned **28 from
23,284**.

**Then doubled down on what answered.** Roofing worked, so it ran again. Lawyers
worked in Texas, so they ran in Florida. Water restoration, plumbing and the second
roofing batch returned nothing, and that was the signal to stop rather than push.

## Problem three: one of the inbox pools was dead

![After 23 July pool A returned 226 replies and 13 interested from 14,219 sends while pool B returned 15 replies and none from 7,433](/assets/exhibit-7-pool-split.svg)

Pool B returned 220 replies in July. Through August it sent 7,433 and returned
fifteen. Same campaigns, same lists, same days as Pool A.

**A delivery failure, not a market one.** And invisible on any reply-rate chart.
It also means plumbing's zero is partly an artefact, because half that volume never
arrived.

## What I would keep

Run the angles in parallel. Split the infrastructure before you have a question
about it. Let the zeros stay visible: the 5,508 sends that produced nothing are the
reason the 28 is believable.
