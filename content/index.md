What I am good at is narrower and more useful than "outbound": **finding the moment a
company becomes worth contacting, getting the data to reach them that does not exist in any
vendor's database, and then proving what it did.** A list says who looks like a customer. A
signal says who is in the market this week, and almost everything on this page starts there.

Every number on this page is pulled from a client-facing dashboard or a sending platform's
API, with its definition and its date attached, because a number that dies under the
follow-up question was never worth stating. Clients are described rather than named, they
are a partner's clients, not mine to name, and every figure belongs to the single account
it is attributed to. **Nothing here is a composite, an aggregate, or a rounded-up estimate.**
