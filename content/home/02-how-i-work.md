Every case study on this site is written to the same three questions, because they are the
three a hiring manager actually asks.

**What was broken.** Not the brief, the real problem. On more than one account those were
different things, and the gap between them was where the work was. A brief that says "we need
more leads" and a funnel that says "your copy is addressed to the wrong people" is the most
common version of it.

**What I built.** The system, the tools, the logic, and the judgement calls inside it, including
the ones that turned out wrong. A list is not a deliverable; a list with a scoring column and an
honest verdict is.

**What changed.** A number, and the definition that number depends on. *Sent* counts emails, not
people. *Positive* is a flag a human sets during triage, so it is a floor rather than a measured
rate. I put both caveats next to the figure every time, because a number that cannot survive a
follow-up question is worth less than no number.

Every case study also carries a **what I would do differently** section. Three of them describe
something that shipped broken. That is deliberate: an account with no failures in it has either
been curated or not measured.
