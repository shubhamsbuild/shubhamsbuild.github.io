### GTM Engineer · a B2B outbound agency · 2025 – present
Ran the go-to-market data layer for **thirteen client accounts simultaneously**, across tax
advisory, legal, PR, government-contracting consulting, higher-ed SaaS, DTC e-commerce, capital
markets, insurance, automotive SaaS and loyalty software. Owned the whole surface per account:
ICP definition, list acquisition, enrichment architecture, sending infrastructure, copy strategy,
and the measurement that decided what to keep.

**336 campaigns · 1,121,622 emails · 957 positive replies.** Several of those accounts were
inherited mid-flight and rebuilt; several were built from an empty workspace, including domain
and inbox provisioning from zero.

The work that was genuinely mine to invent sits in three places: **data acquisition** for markets
no vendor covers, **deliverability** as an engineering discipline rather than a checklist, and
**measurement** honest enough to kill a client's own idea before it shipped.

### Before that
Cold outbound execution and client-facing account work across the same agency's book, which is
