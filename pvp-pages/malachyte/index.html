2. A weekly paid-traffic landing feed

Scan Meta Ad Library for Shopify apparel and footwear brands, open every ad's destination, and flag 404s, homepage redirects and sold-out pages. Accounts with a live finding go to the rep queue with the ad ID, screenshot and a drafted email.
